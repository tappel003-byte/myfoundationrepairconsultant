Charlie Munger’s line is useful in foundation repair because the outcome often matches what the visit was built to produce.

This page explains how compensation and metrics shape what a homeowner hears — without treating every commissioned inspector as dishonest.

What an incentive is in this industry

If the only way a company is paid is a signed repair contract, the visit is not a neutral exam. It can still be competent. It is not independent.

Commission, close-rate targets, and average-ticket goals are not secret. They are how many national operators staff the first appointment.

What that does not automatically mean

  • The inspector invented the crack
  • Piers are never appropriate
  • A salaried engineer is always right

It means you should separate what was observed from what was sold.

Questions the incentive does not like

  • Is the movement still happening?
  • What was measured besides a walk-through?
  • What would you recommend if you were not allowed to sell piers today?
  • What does the scope exclude?

A final note

This page is educational, not diagnostic. Incentives explain patterns. They do not replace a look at the house.
