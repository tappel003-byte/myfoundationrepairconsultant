Paint, tape, caulk, and trim move less than the house behind them. That is why finishes are early reporters and easy to over-read.

What a finish is telling you

A crack in a finish means that location took more strain than the material could hide. It does not name the soil, the year, or the method.

How to use finishes without panicking

  • Note where the crack is — joint, opening, mid-span
  • Note whether it returns after a clean repair
  • Note whether anything else in the house moves with it

What a finish crack does not automatically mean

  • Structural failure
  • Active settlement
  • A reason to skip time and measurement

Related dictionary terms

  • Cosmetic Crack
  • Hairline Crack
  • Structural Crack
  • Settlement
  • Differential Settlement

(Each of these terms is explained in the MFRC Foundation Dictionary.)

A final note

This page is educational, not diagnostic. Finishes are instruments. They are not the diagnosis.
